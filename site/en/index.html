Get Stow to save it for real

This is a demo; nothing is actually extracted. Video info is fetched by your browser directly from YouTube, and the link you enter isn't sent anywhere else.

Quick extract

From any app, ⌥⌘Y

Press the shortcut and a small panel opens with the link from your clipboard already loaded. Hit Return to queue it with your current settings and the panel closes. Change the format and folder with the chips at the bottom.

Audio and video

Audio or video, your way.

Choose audio or video as the type. Audio saves as MP3, WAV, or FLAC; video as editor-friendly MP4 (up to 1080p) or best quality (up to 4K). Video isn't re-encoded, so you keep the original quality.

Queue and playlists

Pick what you need, audio or video, all at once.

Add as many as you like and they're saved one after another, as audio or video. For a playlist, check just the videos you want and they're saved together in a folder named after the playlist.

Audio in three formats

MP3 at 128, 192, or 320 kbps, WAV, or FLAC, with title and artist metadata embedded.

Video in two formats

MP4 opens right away in Premiere, Final Cut, and QuickTime; best quality goes up to 4K and beyond. Pick the resolution, too.

Paste and go

Title, channel, duration, and thumbnail load as soon as you paste a link.

Safe saving

Never overwrites a file with the same name; it adds a number instead. Remembers your folder and format.

Stays up to date

New app versions are offered in-app, and the extraction engine (yt-dlp) updates itself when YouTube changes.

Feels at home

Looks like each system's own Settings app, with light and dark mode and full keyboard control. VoiceOver support on Mac.

Free and open source

Released under the MIT License. No ads, no sign-ups.

Runs on your Mac

No account, no server. Audio and video are processed on your Mac and saved to the folder you choose.

Install

Allow it once, and you're set.

Stow isn't code-signed by Apple or Microsoft yet, so your system asks you to confirm the first time you open it.

Mac

  1. Download Stow-<version>.dmg from Releases, open it, and drag Stow into Applications.
  2. macOS 15 or later: opening Stow shows a warning. Click "Done", then go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click "Open Anyway". Open Stow again and click "Open".
  3. macOS 14: Control-click Stow in Applications → "Open" → click "Open" in the warning.
  4. After that it opens normally, and new versions install from inside the app.

Windows

  1. Download Stow-win-x64-Setup.exe from the Windows release and run it. For ARM PCs such as Snapdragon, use Stow-win-arm64-Setup.exe.
  2. If "Windows protected your PC" appears, click More info → Run anyway.
  3. It installs to your account without admin rights and adds Stow to the Start menu. Uninstall it from Settings → Apps.
  4. The Windows version is 0.1. Quick extract and automatic updates come in the next version.
